# Security Policy

## 🛡️ Our Commitment
The A.R.C. team takes security vulnerabilities seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and we will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

##  reporting-a-vulnerability Reporting a Vulnerability

**DO NOT report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**

Instead, please report them to us privately. This allows us to fix the vulnerability before it is widely known.

Please send an email to **dgtalbug@gmail.com** with "A.R.C. Vulnerability Report" in the subject line.

In your report, please include:
* **A detailed description** of the vulnerability.
* **Steps to reproduce** the vulnerability.
* **The affected component** (all public repos).
* **Your name/handle** [dgtalbug/@dgtalbug].

## 🕥 Our Process
1. We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report within **48 hours**.
2. We will investigate the report and determine its impact.
3. We will work to create a patch and a release timeline.
4. We will notify you when the patch is released and, if you wish, publicly credit you for the discovery.

Thank you for helping keep A.R.C. secure.
